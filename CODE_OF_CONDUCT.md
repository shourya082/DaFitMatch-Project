# Community standards

DaFitMatch welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help keep discussions safe, respectful, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept feedback in good faith and focus on what helps the project and its users.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and other conduct that creates a hostile or unsafe environment are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Include a link or context and a concise description. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. The maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
